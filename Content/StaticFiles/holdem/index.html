HoldEm turns a real table into a No-Limit Texas Hold'em night with your friends. The tablet in the middle deals; every phone holds its player's two cards.

How it works

Put a tablet in the middle of the table: it is the board and the dealer. Everyone else opens HoldEm on their phone, joins in seconds over the same Wi-Fi (or by scanning the table's QR code) and sees their two private cards on their own screen, face down until they hold them.

The app shuffles, deals, keeps every stack, bet, pot and side pot exact, and moves the pot to the winner, so nobody counts chips or argues about the pot. The talking, the bluffing and the reading of faces stay at the table.

What you get

  • Cash game or tournament, with blinds that can rise on a schedule
  • Rules like real No-Limit Hold'em, side pots and showdowns included
  • All-in run-outs that turn the hands over one by one, with the chance to win after every card
  • Up to ten players, on iPhone, iPad and Android at the same table
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no internet: the table runs on your local Wi-Fi
  • Table looks, card backs, card faces and more in the shop, seen by everyone at the table
Play chips only. HoldEm is a simulated card game for adults. Chips have no value, cannot be bought and cannot be exchanged for anything. There is no real-money gambling and there are no prizes.
