This toy needs WebGL 2, which this browser does not offer.

How to Play

Drag sideways
Spin the base. Flick it hard and see what happens.
Drag up or down
Tilt the view
Pinch or scroll
Zoom
Double-tap
Reset the view
Tap
Poke the head, the belly or the base
Keys
Arrows spin and tilt, + and - zoom, W wave, J jump, D dance, S scan, M sound, 0 reset
