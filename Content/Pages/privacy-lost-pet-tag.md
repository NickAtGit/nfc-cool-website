## What this policy covers

This policy covers:

<ul>
<li>the Lost Pet Tag by NFC.cool app for iPhone and for Android ("the app");</li>
<li>its App Clip on iPhone, which opens a pet's page without installing the app;</li>
<li>the pages at pets.nfc.cool: your pet's page, the page a finder writes from, the records you share and the invitations you send.</li>
</ul>

A Lost Pet Tag account is separate from an NFC.cool Business Card account, even if you use the same email address. NFC.cool Business Card, NFC.cool Tools and the nfc.cool website have their own [privacy policies](/privacy/).

## The data we process, and why

### Your account

You need an account to add a pet. You create it with your email address and a password, or on iPhone with Sign in with Apple. We store:

<ul>
<li>your email address, and a user name made from it, which the app does not show;</li>
<li>your password, stored only as a salted hash, never as text;</li>
<li>if you use Sign in with Apple: the user identifier Apple gives us and the email address Apple shares, which can be a private relay address. The app asks Apple only for your email address;</li>
<li>whether you confirmed your email address, when you joined and when you last signed in;</li>
<li>the identifier of your purchases at RevenueCat (see <a href="#purchases">Purchases</a>).</li>
</ul>

The app keeps you signed in with a short access token and a longer refresh token. On iPhone they are kept in the iOS Keychain. On Android they are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and the app is excluded from Android backups.

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to give you an account, sign you in and keep it secure. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Your pets and their records

For each pet you add, we store what you enter:

<ul>
<li>its name, species, breed, photo and a note for finders;</li>
<li>a phone number, if you add one;</li>
<li>whether it is lost, since when, where it was last seen, and whether a finder may see your phone number and its health notes while it is lost;</li>
<li>with a paid plan, its health records: vaccines (with dates, vet, batch number and an optional reminder), medication (with dose and schedule), allergies and conditions, vet visits (with notes), weight, and documents such as vaccination certificates, as PDF files or pictures.</li>
</ul>

Before we store your pet's photo, we make it smaller and save it again as a new picture. The new picture leaves out the photo's metadata, such as where and when it was taken. Documents are made smaller where possible and kept in private storage.

Health records are about your pet. Whatever you write in them, such as a vet's name or a note, is stored as you write it.

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to keep your pet's profile and records for you, and to show finders what you choose. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Your pet's tag and page

The app writes a link to an NFC tag, or shows it as a QR code you can save, share or print. The link has the form pets.nfc.cool/t/ followed by a random code. The tag holds no name, address or phone number, and it does not track your pet. We only learn where your pet is if a finder chooses to tell you.

Anyone who taps the tag, scans the QR code or opens the link sees your pet's page:

<ul>
<li>your pet's name, species, breed, photo and your note for finders;</li>
<li>the allergies, conditions and current medication you marked as visible, with dose and schedule. You can hide them while your pet is lost;</li>
<li>while your pet is lost: that it is missing and since when, where it was last seen, and your phone number, but only if you chose to show it.</li>
</ul>

The page never shows your name or your email address. It tells search engines not to index it. On an iPhone, the page can open in the App Clip, or in the app if it is installed.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Showing the page is what the tag is for.

### If you found a pet

You do not need the app or an account. On the pet's page, or in the App Clip, you can send its owner a message:

<ul>
<li><strong>Your message</strong> is required, up to 2,000 characters.</li>
<li><strong>How to reach you</strong> is optional: a phone number or email address, as you type it.</li>
<li><strong>Where you are</strong> is optional. The box "Share where I am with the owner" is offered while the pet is lost, or if its owner has a paid plan. It starts unticked. Your phone or browser asks for your location only when you tick it, and we receive it only when you send the message.</li>
</ul>

We deliver your message, with your contact details and location if you added them, to the pet's owner and the people who share the pet with them (see <a href="#family-and-shared-records">Family and shared records</a>). They read it in the app. On iPhone the app shows your location on an Apple Maps map, and they can open it in their maps app. Their notification says only that a message arrived, not what it says. What the pet's owners do with your details after that is up to them.

To limit abuse, we use your IP address to work out a code that is different for every pet. We store that code with your message, not your IP address. It limits how many messages can be sent from one address, and lets the owner block a sender. A blocked sender is not told, and their next messages are not stored. An owner can also delete or report a message.

<strong>Retention:</strong> we delete messages, with their contact details and location, after 90 days. The pet's owners can delete them sooner.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> for your message and contact details, our legitimate interest, the owner's and yours in getting a lost pet home (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For your location, your consent, given when you tick the box (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). To have a message deleted after you sent it, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool with the tag's link and the time you sent it.

### Taps and alerts

Each time someone opens a pet's page, we record that the tag was opened, when, and whether on the web, in the App Clip or in the app. This record holds no IP address, no device identifier and nothing else about the person. Link previews and the pet's own people are not counted.

The pet's owners see these taps in the app while the pet is lost, or with a paid plan, and then get a notification when the tag is opened, at most once a minute. We delete taps after 90 days.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Family and shared records

<ul>
<li><strong>Co-owners.</strong> With a paid plan, a pet's owner can invite others to share the pet. An invitation is a link that works once, for 7 days. Co-owners see and manage the pet, its records, messages and taps. The people who share a pet see each other's name, or the email address of an account that has no name.</li>
<li><strong>Shared records.</strong> With a paid plan, you can make a read-only link to your pet's records for a vet or a sitter. You choose which sections it shows, and it works until you end it or until the date you choose. Anyone with the link can see those sections. Documents in it open through links that work for an hour.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Purchases

The app is free to use, with a paid plan you buy through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google takes the payment, and we never see your payment details.

The app uses RevenueCat to manage purchases. RevenueCat knows you by a random identifier that our server gives your account, not by your email address. Before you sign in, RevenueCat uses an anonymous identifier. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active, regularly and when RevenueCat tells us something changed. We store whether your plan is active, when it ends and when we last checked.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app gives our server a token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service. We use it to tell you:

<ul>
<li>that a finder sent a message about your pet;</li>
<li>that your pet's tag was opened, while it is lost or with a paid plan;</li>
<li>that a vaccine is due in two weeks, if you set a reminder.</li>
</ul>

A notification includes your pet's name, and for a vaccine its name and due date. It does not include a finder's message. It passes through Firebase and, on iPhone, Apple's push service. A token is deleted when you sign out, when Firebase reports that it no longer works, and with your account.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

### Emails we send you

We send emails about your account: a link to confirm your email address when you sign up, password resets, and warnings before we delete an account that has been inactive (see <a href="#how-long-we-keep-data">How long we keep data</a>). They are sent through Mailjet.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### On your phone

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it.

On iPhone:

<ul>
<li><strong>NFC:</strong> to write your pet's link to a tag.</li>
<li><strong>Photos:</strong> to choose a photo of your pet or a health document, and to save your pet's QR code.</li>
<li><strong>Location</strong> (app and App Clip): only when you, as a finder, tick "Share where I am with the owner".</li>
<li><strong>Notifications:</strong> see <a href="#notifications">Notifications</a>.</li>
</ul>

On Android:

<ul>
<li><strong>NFC:</strong> to write your pet's link to a tag.</li>
<li><strong>Notifications:</strong> see <a href="#notifications">Notifications</a>.</li>
</ul>

On Android you pick photos and documents with Android's own picker, which needs no permission. The Android app does not ask for your location.

The apps contain no advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools. The iPhone app never asks for permission to track you, and the Android app removes the permission to use your advertising ID. The App Clip contains none of these tools, and no purchase or notification tools either.

You can withdraw a permission at any time in your phone's settings.

### Technical data, errors and security

<ul>
<li><strong>The finder's side.</strong> On pets.nfc.cool, and on the requests the App Clip and the app send to show a pet's page or send a message, our request log leaves out the IP address, the browser and the account, and masks the tag's code. The web server keeps no access log for pets.nfc.cool.</li>
<li><strong>Your side.</strong> For your own requests, such as signing in or editing your pet, our server logs each request with the address requested, the time, the result, the IP address and browser, and your account number. The logs stay on our server and are not shared.</li>
<li><strong>Error reports.</strong> We use Sentry to find and fix errors on our server. On the finder's side, an error report keeps only the address requested, with codes masked, and the kind of request. Sentry is not sent IP addresses or cookies.</li>
<li><strong>Rate limits.</strong> We use IP addresses in short-lived counters to limit requests, for example to the finder's pages.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to run the service securely and fix problems. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Who receives data

We share data only with the people and services below, and only for the reasons given in this policy.

<ul>
<li><strong>Anyone who opens your pet's link</strong> sees what you chose to show on your pet's page. Anyone with a shared record's link sees the sections you chose.</li>
<li><strong>The pet's owners and co-owners</strong> receive finders' messages, with contact details and location if the finder added them, and see taps.</li>
<li><strong>DigitalOcean:</strong> hosts our server, database and files, and delivers files through its content delivery network.</li>
<li><strong>Cloudflare:</strong> protects DigitalOcean's content delivery network against bots (see <a href="#cookies-and-similar-technologies">Cookies and similar technologies</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Mailjet:</strong> sends our emails.</li>
<li><strong>Google (Firebase):</strong> delivers notifications.</li>
<li><strong>Apple:</strong> Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, push delivery on iPhone, the App Clip, and the Apple Maps map in the iPhone app.</li>
<li><strong>Google Play:</strong> purchases on Android.</li>
<li><strong>RevenueCat:</strong> manages in-app purchases.</li>
<li><strong>Sentry:</strong> error reports from our server.</li>
</ul>

We do not sell personal data.

## Where your data is stored, and data outside the EU

Our server, database and files are hosted by DigitalOcean in its data centres in the New York area, USA. Mailjet stores emails in the EU, in Germany and Belgium.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, each transfer rests on one of these safeguards:

<ul>
<li><strong>The EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.</strong> The European Commission decided that US companies certified under it protect personal data adequately (Art. 45 GDPR).</li>
<li><strong>Standard contractual clauses.</strong> The European Commission's model clauses are part of our contract with the service (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).</li>
</ul>

For each service:

<ul>
<li><strong>DigitalOcean, LLC</strong> (USA): server, database and files. Data Privacy Framework, with standard contractual clauses in its data processing agreement in case the framework stops applying.</li>
<li><strong>Functional Software, Inc.</strong> (Sentry, USA): error reports. Data Privacy Framework, with standard contractual clauses as a fallback.</li>
<li><strong>Google LLC</strong> (Firebase, USA): Data Privacy Framework, with standard contractual clauses in Google's terms.</li>
<li><strong>Cloudflare, Inc.</strong> (USA): Data Privacy Framework.</li>
<li><strong>Apple Inc.</strong> (USA): standard contractual clauses.</li>
<li><strong>RevenueCat, Inc.</strong> (USA): standard contractual clauses.</li>
</ul>

To get a copy of the standard contractual clauses that apply, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool. The European Commission explains these safeguards on its [information page](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection_en).

## How long we keep data

<ul>
<li><strong>Your account:</strong> until you delete it, or until we delete it because it has been inactive (see below).</li>
<li><strong>Your pets and their records:</strong> until you delete them or your account. If someone shares the pet with you, it stays with them (see <a href="#export-your-data-and-delete-your-account">Export your data and delete your account</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Finders' messages, with contact details and location, and taps:</strong> 90 days, or until the pet's owners delete them.</li>
<li><strong>Blocked senders:</strong> the code that blocks a sender stays until the pet is deleted.</li>
<li><strong>Invitations and shared records:</strong> an invitation works for 7 days, a shared record until you end it or until the date you set. Their records stay with the pet until the pet is deleted.</li>
<li><strong>Tag codes:</strong> when a pet is deleted, its tags keep only their codes, linked to no one, so the page says the tag is no longer active and no code is given out twice.</li>
<li><strong>Your plan:</strong> with your account. Your customer record at RevenueCat stays until you ask us to remove it.</li>
<li><strong>Notification tokens:</strong> until you sign out, until Firebase reports that they no longer work, or until you delete your account.</li>
<li><strong>Server logs:</strong> they are rotated, so old entries are deleted as new ones arrive.</li>
<li><strong>Rate limit counters:</strong> at most an hour.</li>
<li><strong>Backups:</strong> deleted data can stay in our database backups until they are replaced.</li>
<li><strong>Purchase records at Apple and Google:</strong> under their own policies.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Inactive accounts.</strong> We never delete an account for inactivity while it has a pet, as owner or co-owner, because a tag has to work the day a pet goes missing. An account with no pet that nobody has signed in to for a year gets an email warning that it will be deleted in 30 days. A final warning follows 3 days before. If the account is still inactive then, we delete it in the same way as when you delete it yourself.

## What you must give us, and automated decisions

To create an account you need an email address and a password, or Sign in with Apple. Without them we cannot give you an account. Everything you put in your pet's profile is up to you. As a finder, you only need to write a message. No law requires you to give us personal data.

We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

<ul>
<li>get a copy of your data and information about how we use it (Art. 15);</li>
<li>have wrong data corrected (Art. 16);</li>
<li>have your data deleted (Art. 17);</li>
<li>have processing restricted (Art. 18);</li>
<li>receive your data in a machine-readable format and take it elsewhere (Art. 20);</li>
<li>withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before (Art. 7(3)).</li>
</ul>

<strong>Your right to object.</strong> Where we rely on legitimate interests (delivering finders' messages, server logs, error reports and rate limits), you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Email info (at) nfc (dot) cool.

Much of this you can do yourself in the app, as described below. For anything else, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool. We answer within one month. If you sent a message as a finder, include the tag's link, so we can find it.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, in the EU country where you live or work or where you think your rights were infringed. In Portugal, where we are based, that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados ([cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt)).

## Export your data and delete your account

<strong>Export.</strong> In the app's settings, Export gives you a file with your pets: their details, tags, health records, the list of their documents, and their messages and taps. For a copy of your account details, email us.

<strong>Delete your account.</strong> You can delete your account in the app's settings, or by emailing info (at) nfc (dot) cool. When you delete it:

<ul>
<li>each pet you own goes to the person who has shared it with you the longest, who becomes its owner. A pet that nobody shares with you is deleted, with its records, documents, messages and taps;</li>
<li>you leave the pets other people share with you;</li>
<li>we delete your account, your plan record, your sign-in sessions and your notification tokens;</li>
<li>the tags of deleted pets show a page that says they are no longer active.</li>
</ul>

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account settings. To have your customer record at RevenueCat deleted, email us.

## Children

You must be at least 16 to create an account. The app is not directed at children.

Anyone who finds a pet can use its page, whatever their age, and no account is needed. A message is enough to help. A finder does not have to give a phone number, an email address or their location.

If you think someone younger than 16 has given us personal data, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool and we will delete it.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The pages at pets.nfc.cool need no sign-in, and our server sets no cookies on them. They use no analytics and no advertising. The pages' files and your pet's photo are delivered by the content delivery network of our hosting provider, DigitalOcean. Cloudflare, which protects that network against bots, can set a cookie called __cf_bm, which tells people from bots and lasts 30 minutes. This is needed for the pages to work, which is why we do not ask for consent.

The app stores on your phone what it needs to work: your sign-in, the identifier RevenueCat uses for your purchases, and your notification token.

## Security

All connections to our servers are encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Health documents are kept in private storage and open only through short-lived signed links. Pet pages and shared records tell search engines not to index them, and tell browsers not to keep a copy. On the finder's side we do not log IP addresses.

Your pet's photo is shown on its page, so anyone with the tag's link can see it.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and fix problems quickly.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last modified" date at the top of this page. We do not start to use your data for a new purpose, or share it with a new kind of recipient, before this page describes it.
