## How It Works

<div class="page-cards-grid">
<article class="page-card"><h3>1. Point it at what you have</h3><p>Your website, your Google listing, your Instagram, a link page or a few photos. NFC.cool reads them and fills in your menu, hours, photos and contact details. Or start from scratch.</p></article>
<article class="page-card"><h3>2. Pick a style for your kind of business</h3><p>Twelve styles, from Counter for cafés and bakeries to Chair for salons and Trade for plumbers and builders.</p></article>
<article class="page-card"><h3>3. Share it</h3><p>Put an NFC tag or a QR code on your counter, your tables or your door, or share the link. Customers open your page in the browser, with nothing to install.</p></article>

Your first page is free. A plan decides what your customers can do on it, paid monthly or yearly. Prices include VAT.

<h2 class="landing-section-title">Pages FAQ</h2>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What is a page, and how is it different from a business card?</summary>
<p>A business card is one person's contact details. A page is your business: your menu, opening hours, photos, and a way for customers to book, order or ask for a quote. Both live in the NFC.cool Business Card app.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do my customers need an app?</summary>
<p>No. Your page opens in the browser on any phone or computer, and so does ordering at the table.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What can I do for free?</summary>
<p>Your first page is free, with every block, your menu and your photos. Customers can already send requests. They wait for you for 7 days until you pick a plan, and a free page shows "Made with NFC.cool".</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How does the free trial work?</summary>
<p>On the web, every plan starts with 14 days free. The trial needs a payment card, and you're billed when it ends unless you cancel. Each person gets one trial.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can I pay in the app?</summary>
<p>Yes, in the App Store or on Google Play, or on the web. The app shows its price in your currency.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do I need NFC tags?</summary>
<p>No. A QR code or a link works too. If you want something customers can tap, any writable NFC tag works, like a sticker on your counter or your tables.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can I have more than one page?</summary>
<p>Yes. Your first page is free, and every further page has its own plan.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What about pages for a team?</summary>
<p>A team's pages go on the team's bill, at 20% less at every level, from {{PRICE:team_page:month}} a month. See <a href="/business-card/teams/">Business Card for Teams</a>.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What happens if I cancel?</summary>
<p>Your page stays online. New requests wait for you again, "Made with NFC.cool" comes back and table ordering stops. Every request you already received stays readable and you can still answer it.</p>
</details>
