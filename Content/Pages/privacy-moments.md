## What this policy covers

This policy covers Moments by NFC.cool, called Momento Marks on the App Store:

<ul>
<li>the app for iPhone and iPad, with its share extension, widget and controls ("the app");</li>
<li>its App Clip, which opens an album without installing the app;</li>
<li>the album pages and your account pages at tap.nfc.cool, and the service behind them at api.nfc.cool.</li>
</ul>

A Moments account is separate from any other NFC.cool account. NFC.cool Business Card, NFC.cool Tools and the nfc.cool website have their own [privacy policies](/privacy/).

## The data we process, and why

### Your account

You need an account to put an album online, buy one, or own one. You make it with your email address and a password. We store:

<ul>
<li>your email address, and your password only as a hash made with Argon2id, never as text;</li>
<li>the name and profile photo you add, if you add them. A profile photo is saved again as a new picture, without its metadata;</li>
<li>when you confirmed your email address, with the code we email you or a link we sent you;</li>
<li>when you accepted the terms, and which version, where the app sends it;</li>
<li>your notification settings;</li>
<li>your sign-in sessions: when each started and was last used, and the device as your browser or the app describes itself.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to give you an account, sign you in and keep it secure. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Albums

An album holds its title, its settings, a cover, and its owners and administrators. A password, if you set one, is stored only as a hash. Who can see an album depends on the settings its owner chooses.

An album you make on your phone without putting it online is stored only on your phone. The app writes it without using the network.

If you are signed in and open someone else's album through its tag or link, we remember which album you opened and when you first and last opened it, so it appears in your library on your devices.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Memories

A memory is a photo, a video, a voice memo or a note that you add to an album, with its caption, the look you chose, the time it was taken, where it was taken, and who added it. In an album that needs review, a memory appears once an owner or administrator approves it.

<ul>
<li><strong>Photos:</strong> we keep the file you sent, unchanged, in private storage. Nobody browsing the album is ever given that file. What people see are copies we make. The larger copies carry the photo's metadata as your file had it, such as the camera, the time and the coordinates. The small tiles carry only the location.</li>
<li><strong>Videos:</strong> we keep the file as it arrives and play it back from it. On your phone, the app can make a video smaller before it sends it, depending on the album's plan. The video keeps its metadata.</li>
<li><strong>Voice memos:</strong> the copy people play has its metadata removed.</li>
</ul>

Anyone who can see an album can save the copies it shows. An owner can download an original, and owners and administrators can export the whole album, on the full-quality plan. An export can be shared with a link.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Where a memory was made

<ul>
<li>When the camera is open, and you allowed the app to use your location, the app adds where you are to what you take.</li>
<li>When you add a photo or video from your library or the share sheet, the app uses the location stored in the file, if it has one. Our server also reads the location and time stored in a file you upload, if the app or page did not send them.</li>
<li>Your iPhone looks up the place name with Apple Maps, which receives the coordinates for that.</li>
</ul>

The place is shown with the memory as a place name. The coordinates are part of the album's data that the app receives, so anyone who can see the album can get them. The larger copies of a photo carry them too, and so does an album export. Once a memory is in an album, the only way to remove its location is to delete the memory. If you would rather a memory did not carry a location, decide before you add it: you can turn off location for the app in your iPhone's settings, and your iPhone can leave the location out when you share a photo.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> for your phone's location, your consent, which you give when you allow the app to use it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). This is also the consent Portuguese law requires before an app reads information on your phone (Art. 5 of Lei 41/2004). You can withdraw it in your iPhone's settings. For the location in a file you upload, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Guests

You do not need an account to add to someone's album. A guest gives a name, at least two letters, and can add a profile photo, which is saved again without its metadata. We create a guest profile with an identifier and a secret. On the web, the secret is kept in a cookie. In the App Clip and the app, it is kept on your iPhone.

A guest profile also holds your notification settings, your settings for each album, and the notification tokens of your devices (see <a href="#notifications">Notifications</a>). If you later make an account, your guest profile is linked to it.

You can delete your guest profile in the app, or on the web on your guest page. That removes your name and profile photo. What you added to albums stays there until it is deleted, as described in <a href="#how-long-we-keep-data">How long we keep data</a>.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. A name shows the album's owner and guests who added each memory.

### Comments, reactions, reports and blocks

People who can see an album can comment and react, if the album allows it. We store each comment and reaction with its author.

Anyone can report a memory, a comment or a profile, with a reason if they want. We do not store who made the report with it. A reported memory goes back to review, and a reported profile photo is hidden. An album's owner can block a person from the album, which removes what they added there. NFC.cool staff can open an album and its memories to deal with reports and abuse.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> our legitimate interest, and that of the people in an album, in keeping albums safe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Tags and taps

An album can have NFC tags, each with a label its owner can give it. When a tag opens an album, we record that it was opened, when, and whether by NFC, QR code or link. This record has no column for an IP address, a browser or a person. It helps an owner find out which of their tags is which. The first time a tag is tapped, the album's owners are told.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Gifts

An owner can give an album to someone by entering their email address. We store that address, email the person a link to accept, which works for 14 days, and tell the giver whether it was accepted, declined or expired, with the recipient's address.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> our and the giver's legitimate interest in delivering a gift the giver chose to send (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Purchases

You buy an album, or a subscription that covers your albums, through the App Store. Apple takes the payment. We do not receive your card number, your billing address or any other payment details.

<ul>
<li>The app uses RevenueCat to make and manage purchases. RevenueCat knows you by an identifier made from your account's identifier, not by your email address. Before you sign in, RevenueCat uses an anonymous identifier.</li>
<li>The app sends us Apple's signed record of the purchase. We store it as Apple issued it, with its identifiers: the transaction, the product, the environment, the dates, and any refund with its reason. We keep them with your account and the album they paid for.</li>
<li>We also store the notifications Apple and RevenueCat send us about purchases, as they arrive.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We keep purchase records after the album or your account is deleted, so payments and refunds stay accountable. For that, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app gives us a token from Apple's push service, with your device's language, time zone, the app version, and whether it is the app or the App Clip. The App Clip can show notifications for up to 8 hours after you open it without asking, and for longer if you allow it.

We send notifications about your albums, for example new memories to review, comments, reactions and gifts. A notification can include the album's title, the name of the person who acted, the start of a comment, a reaction, a tag's label, or for a gift the giver's name or the recipient's email address. It passes through Apple's push service. We also keep your notifications in the app's inbox. A token is deleted when you sign out, when Apple reports that it no longer works, and with your account.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can choose which notifications you get in the app, and turn them off in your iPhone's settings.

### Emails we send you

We send emails through Mailjet: sign-in and password reset links, the code that confirms your email address, reminders of memories waiting for review, notice that an album you own was deleted (with the email address of the owner who deleted it) and when it will be removed for good, a link when an album export is ready, and emails about gifts.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and for emails to a gift's recipient, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR as described in <a href="#gifts">Gifts</a>.

### Features from Apple

<ul>
<li><strong>Maps:</strong> place names, the album map and map images come from Apple Maps, which receives the coordinates they show.</li>
<li><strong>Image Playground:</strong> to reimagine a photo or make art for a poster, the app opens Apple's Image Playground, which Apple provides under its own terms. If you choose a style from an outside provider there, Apple sends your photo to that provider, under Apple's and that provider's terms. The app does not send it there itself.</li>
<li><strong>Recognising objects and Visual Intelligence:</strong> you can link an object to an album, so pointing the camera at it opens the album. The recognition runs on your iPhone. For an album that is online, the app also uploads a numerical description of the object, made by Apple's Vision framework, and a label if you give one, so it works on your other devices. The photo of the object stays on your iPhone.</li>
<li><strong>The App Clip</strong> is delivered by Apple when you tap a tag or scan a code.</li>
</ul>

### On your device

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it:

<ul>
<li><strong>NFC:</strong> to open an album from a tag and to write tags.</li>
<li><strong>Camera:</strong> to take photos and videos, scan codes, and recognise objects you linked to an album, on your iPhone.</li>
<li><strong>Microphone:</strong> to record voice memos and the sound of videos.</li>
<li><strong>Photos:</strong> to save what you take with quick capture, and the memories you choose to save, to your library. Picking a photo needs no permission.</li>
<li><strong>Location:</strong> see <a href="#where-a-memory-was-made">Where a memory was made</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Face ID:</strong> to show album passwords saved on your iPhone.</li>
<li><strong>Notifications:</strong> see <a href="#notifications">Notifications</a>.</li>
</ul>

The App Clip asks for the camera, the microphone and location for the same reasons.

The app keeps your library, offline copies of albums and its caches on your iPhone. Albums kept only on your phone are part of your iPhone's backup. Album passwords and tag keys are kept in your Keychain, and sync through iCloud Keychain if you use it. Apart from your iPhone's own backup, the app does not store your albums in iCloud.

The app and the App Clip contain no advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools, and never ask for permission to track you. You can withdraw a permission at any time in your iPhone's settings.

### Technical data and security

<ul>
<li><strong>Request logs:</strong> our servers log each request with the address requested, the IP address and the result. The logs are capped in size, so old entries are deleted as new ones arrive. They are not shared.</li>
<li><strong>Rate limits:</strong> to stop abuse, such as mass sign-ups or guessed passwords, we count requests by IP address, email address or account. We delete these counters after 25 hours.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to run the service securely. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Who receives data

We share data only with the people and services below, and only for the reasons given in this policy.

<ul>
<li><strong>People who can see an album</strong> see what is in it: memories with their captions, places and authors' names and profile photos, comments and reactions. Owners and administrators also see memories waiting for review. Anyone with an export's link can download it.</li>
<li><strong>Scaleway:</strong> hosts our servers, database and files.</li>
<li><strong>Mailjet:</strong> sends our emails.</li>
<li><strong>Apple:</strong> App Store purchases, push notifications, the App Clip, Apple Maps, and Image Playground with any outside provider you choose there.</li>
<li><strong>RevenueCat:</strong> makes and manages purchases in the app.</li>
</ul>

We do not sell personal data.

## Where your data is stored, and data outside the EU

Our servers, database and files are hosted by Scaleway in its Paris region, in France. Mailjet stores emails in the EU, in Germany and Belgium.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, each transfer rests on one of these safeguards:

<ul>
<li><strong>The EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.</strong> The European Commission decided that US companies certified under it protect personal data adequately (Art. 45 GDPR).</li>
<li><strong>Standard contractual clauses.</strong> The European Commission's model clauses are part of our contract with the service (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).</li>
</ul>

For each service:

<ul>
<li><strong>Apple Inc.</strong> (USA): standard contractual clauses.</li>
<li><strong>RevenueCat, Inc.</strong> (USA): standard contractual clauses.</li>
</ul>

An outside provider you choose in Image Playground receives your photo from Apple, under Apple's terms.

To get a copy of the standard contractual clauses that apply, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool. The European Commission explains these safeguards on its [information page](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection_en).

## How long we keep data

<ul>
<li><strong>Your account:</strong> until you delete it.</li>
<li><strong>Sign-in sessions:</strong> a session ends after about 30 days without use. Ended sessions are deleted 90 days later. Sign-in links work for 15 minutes, password reset links for 60 minutes, and email codes for 24 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Albums:</strong> until they are deleted. A deleted album can be restored for 30 days, then it is removed for good with everything in it.</li>
<li><strong>Memories:</strong> a deleted memory is removed for good after 30 days.</li>
<li><strong>Comments and reactions:</strong> a deleted one disappears at once, and is removed for good with its memory or album.</li>
<li><strong>Guest profiles:</strong> until you delete them. Deleting one removes your name and profile photo.</li>
<li><strong>Album exports:</strong> 7 days, then the file is deleted.</li>
<li><strong>Gifts:</strong> an offer works for 14 days. Its record stays with the album.</li>
<li><strong>Notification tokens:</strong> until you sign out, until Apple reports that they no longer work, or until you delete your account.</li>
<li><strong>Purchase records:</strong> we keep them after the album or your account is deleted, as described in <a href="#purchases">Purchases</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Your customer record at RevenueCat:</strong> to have it deleted, email us.</li>
<li><strong>Rate limit counters:</strong> 25 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Request logs:</strong> they are capped in size, so old entries are deleted as new ones arrive.</li>
<li><strong>Backups:</strong> our database backups are kept for 7 days. Deleted data stays in them until then.</li>
<li><strong>What the app keeps on your iPhone:</strong> until you delete it or the app.</li>
</ul>

## What you must give us, and automated decisions

To make an account you need an email address and a password. To add to an album as a guest you need a name. Everything else you add is up to you. No law requires you to give us personal data.

We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

<ul>
<li>get a copy of your data and information about how we use it (Art. 15);</li>
<li>have wrong data corrected (Art. 16);</li>
<li>have your data deleted (Art. 17);</li>
<li>have processing restricted (Art. 18);</li>
<li>receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format and take it elsewhere (Art. 20);</li>
<li>withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before (Art. 7(3)).</li>
</ul>

<strong>Your right to object.</strong> Where we rely on legitimate interests (reports, blocks and staff review, gifts, purchase records after deletion, request logs and rate limits), you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Email info (at) nfc (dot) cool.

Some of this you can do yourself, as described below. For anything else, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool. We answer within one month. If you added to albums as a guest, tell us the album's link and the name you used, so we can find your data.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, in the EU country where you live or work or where you think your rights were infringed. In Portugal, where we are based, that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados ([cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt)).

## Export your data and delete your account

<strong>Export.</strong> An album's owners and administrators can download the whole album in its settings: its photos, videos and voice memos, and a file with captions, names, places with their coordinates, comments and reactions. The download is kept for 7 days. For a copy of your account data, email us.

<strong>Delete your account.</strong> You can delete your account in the app under your profile, on the web on your profile page, or by emailing info (at) nfc (dot) cool. When you delete it:

<ul>
<li>the albums you own alone are deleted with everything in them, including what other people added;</li>
<li>an album you own with someone else stays with them, and you leave it;</li>
<li>the memories you added to other people's albums are deleted, and your comments and reactions there are removed;</li>
<li>we delete your password, your sign-in links, your devices and notifications, your library, your profile photo and your subscription record, and end your sessions;</li>
<li>a guest profile linked to your account loses its name and profile photo.</li>
</ul>

What stays: your purchase records, as described in <a href="#purchases">Purchases</a>, under a record of the account that no longer holds your email address or name. Notifications other people already received keep the name they showed.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store account settings.

## Children

You must be at least 16 to make an account. We do not check anyone's age. Moments is not directed at children.

Albums of families and events often show children. They are added by the album's owners and guests, and who can see them depends on the album's settings.

If you think an account or a guest profile belongs to someone younger than 16, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool and we will delete it.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The album and account pages at tap.nfc.cool use no advertising or analytics cookies, and load no scripts, fonts or embedded content from other companies. They set only these cookies:

<ul>
<li><strong>mm_session:</strong> keeps you signed in. It ends after about 30 days without use, or when you sign out. The app keeps the same cookie.</li>
<li><strong>mm_guest:</strong> your guest profile on the web. It lasts a year.</li>
<li><strong>mm_album_</strong> followed by the album's identifier: set when you unlock an album with a password, so you are not asked again. It lasts 12 hours.</li>
<li><strong>mm_library_view</strong> and <strong>mm_library_sort:</strong> the layout and order you chose for your library. They last a year.</li>
</ul>

The pages also keep your light or dark theme in your browser's storage, if you choose one. It is not sent to us.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> these cookies are needed for the pages to work as you use them, which is why we do not ask for your consent.

## Security

All connections to our servers are encrypted with HTTPS, and browsers are told to use HTTPS only. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, and album passwords are hashed too. Sign-in links, sessions and guest secrets are stored only as hashes. The files you upload are kept in private storage, and the app and pages receive them through short-lived signed links.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and fix problems quickly.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last modified" date at the top of this page. We do not start to use your data for a new purpose, or share it with a new kind of recipient, before this page describes it.
