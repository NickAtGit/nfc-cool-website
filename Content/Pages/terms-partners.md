## What you earn

You earn **20%** of every payment a team you bring makes on Creem for its NFC.cool Business Card for Teams subscription, for as long as it pays. A founding deal earns **40%** instead, as described in Founding deals.

<ul>
<li>Creem works out your commission under its own rules, from what the team pays on Creem: for example, whether tax, discounts or Creem's fees are taken off first.</li>
<li>We have set up our programme at Creem so that commission follows the team's subscription, monthly or yearly, renewals included, for as long as the team pays. If Creem does not pay commission that these terms describe, tell us and we take it up with Creem.</li>
<li>If the team starts with a free trial, nothing is paid during the trial, so the trial earns nothing. Commission starts with the first payment.</li>
<li>Prices depend on the business's country and currency, so the same team earns a different amount in different countries.</li>
<li>If a payment is refunded or charged back, no commission is owed on it. Creem may take back the commission it earned, under its own rules.</li>
</ul>

## Founding deals

A founding deal is a team you bring that holds one of the programme's founding places. A founding deal earns **40%** instead of 20%, for as long as the team pays.

<ul>
<li><strong>A place belongs to a team, not to a partner.</strong> If you bring 50 founding deals, all 50 earn you 40%.</li>
<li><strong>How many there are.</strong> We start with 100 places. How many are left is shown on the partner page and in your partner area. We may add places at any time. We may also lower the number of places still free, with notice as described in Changes to the programme and these terms. A team that holds a place keeps it.</li>
<li><strong>When a team takes a place.</strong> When the team's checkout opens on Creem and a place is free, the team takes it. We then sell its subscription on our founding product at Creem, for which we have set the commission at 40%. We keep it on the founding product when the team changes its seats or switches between monthly and yearly billing.</li>
<li><strong>Held, then final.</strong> The place is held until the team's first payment. From then on it is the team's for good.</li>
<li><strong>When a held place goes back.</strong> It goes back to the programme if the checkout is not completed within a day, if the trial ends without a payment, or if the first payment is refunded or disputed. If a team whose first payment was refunded or disputed goes on paying, we leave its subscription on the founding product, so it keeps earning 40% while it pays, although its place went back.</li>
<li><strong>A checkout completed late.</strong> If a checkout is completed after its place went back for not being completed in time, the team holds a place again and earns 40%, even if no place is free.</li>
<li><strong>At most 5 held places per partner.</strong> While 5 of your teams hold a place and have not paid yet, the next team you bring is sold on our standard product, at 20%, and stays there. As soon as one of the 5 pays or gives its place back, your next team can take a place again.</li>
<li><strong>Once all places are taken,</strong> new teams earn 20%. Teams that hold a place keep their 40%.</li>
</ul>

## How a team is credited to you

A team is credited to you in one of two ways, and each team is credited to one partner only. Either way, it is credited to you only if you have accepted Creem's invite before the team buys, and only if none of the cases in What never earns a commission applies.

### Your link

Your link is business.nfc.cool/partners/r/ followed by your code. It opens the Teams page on NFC.cool, in the visitor's language.

<ul>
<li>Opening it remembers you in that browser for 30 days from the last time it was opened. If the business then starts a team there and buys it within those 30 days, the team is credited to you.</li>
<li>If the business opens another partner's link later, the last link opened counts.</li>
<li>One opening of your link credits one purchase. For a second team, the business opens your link again.</li>
<li>Your link is remembered by a cookie in the browser. If the business buys in another browser or on another device, or after clearing or blocking cookies, the team cannot be credited to you.</li>
<li>A team that is already credited to a partner stays with that partner.</li>
<li>The demo address in your partner area carries your code, and remembers you in the same way when it is opened.</li>
</ul>

### Setting up a team and handing it over

You set up the team for your client and hand it over. See the next section.

## Setting up a team for a client

<ul>
<li>In your partner area, Set up a team makes a new team in your client's name, credited to you. You are its owner while you build it: its cards, its brand, its members. While you run it, you use it under the Business Card terms. Set up teams only for a real client who asked you to.</li>
<li>When it is ready, you make a take-over link and send it to your client. Only you can make it. It works once, for 7 days. A new link cancels the one before.</li>
<li>You cannot use the take-over link yourself.</li>
<li>When your client takes the team over, they become its only owner and you leave the team. Everything set up while you ran it stops reaching it: other owners and admins become members (your client can promote them again), webhooks stop, other people's access tokens stop working, open invitations and ownership offers are cancelled, and HubSpot and Salesforce are disconnected.</li>
<li>Your client then chooses the seats and pays on Creem. Whether the team gets a free trial first follows the Business Card terms.</li>
<li>A team is handed over once. After that it is never yours to hand over again, and it stays credited to you.</li>
</ul>

The programme is for people who bring real businesses in good faith.

<ul>
<li>Commission is earned only on a payment that these terms credit to you. Commission that comes from breaking these terms or from fraud is not earned: for example a team you bought for yourself, a fake or rewarded sign-up, a click you forced, or a link you shared against the rules.</li>
<li>If we have reason to believe a commission comes from such a breach, we may ask Creem to hold it while we look into it, for at most 60 days. We tell you which commission and why, and you can tell us your side. If the check does not show a breach, we ask Creem to release it.</li>
<li>If such a commission has already been paid to you, you pay it back within 30 days of our written request, which names the commission and the reason. We make such a request within 2 years of the payment of the commission. We never ask for an amount that Creem has already taken back from you, and we never ask for commission on teams that were credited to you correctly.</li>
<li>A team that was credited to you by breaking these terms is no longer credited to you.</li>
</ul>
