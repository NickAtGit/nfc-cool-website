## The data we process, and why

### Your flights and trips

What you add to the app is stored on your iPhone: your flights, seat numbers, booking references, notes, boarding pass images, trips, your home airport and your passport profile.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy in your private iCloud database so it reaches your other devices. Apple stores it under your Apple Account, and we cannot read it. You control this in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to give you the app. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Adding a flight from a boarding pass or document

You can add a flight from a photo, a PDF, a Wallet pass, or by sharing a document to the app. The text is read and understood on your iPhone, with Apple's on-device text recognition and on-device AI model. The document is not sent to us or to an AI provider. AI travel itineraries are made on your iPhone in the same way.

### Looking up flights

When you search for a flight or the app refreshes one, it asks our server for a flight number and date, or for an airport and a time range. Our server gets the answer from AeroDataBox, a flight data service. The request carries no device identifier, and AeroDataBox does not see your IP address.

To limit abuse, our server uses your IP address in a counter that lasts about a minute. It does not store your IP address in a database.

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to show flight information and protect the service. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and for the counter our legitimate interest in a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Live updates and alerts

If you allow notifications, the app registers your iPhone with our server, so that we can reach it. Our server stores:

<ul>
<li>a random device identifier that our server creates;</li>
<li>the push tokens Apple gives the app for notifications and Live Activities;</li>
<li>an App Attest key from Apple, which lets our server check that requests come from the genuine app;</li>
<li>your anonymous RevenueCat identifier (see <a href="#purchases">Purchases</a>), whether 1st Class is active and until when, and when your device last contacted us;</li>
<li>if your free Live Activity has been used, which flight it was used for.</li>
</ul>

For each flight we track live, which means every upcoming flight with a 1st Class subscription and your first flight without one, our server also stores:

<ul>
<li>the flight number, date, scheduled times, airports, cities, time zones and airline;</li>
<li>the push token and timing of that flight's Live Activity;</li>
<li>whether you are flying or following the flight;</li>
<li>what you told the app about it, such as that you have landed or a corrected time.</li>
</ul>

Our server does not receive your name, seat number, booking reference, notes or boarding pass.

We also keep a technical log of what we sent to each device for each flight, so that we can find and fix delivery problems.

Notifications and Live Activities reach your iPhone through Apple's push service.

<strong>Purpose:</strong> to send you gate changes, delays and Live Activity updates. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and for the technical log our legitimate interest in a working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can turn notifications off in your iPhone's settings.

### Purchases

The app is free to use, with a paid 1st Class plan you buy through the App Store. Apple takes the payment, and we never see your payment details.

The app uses RevenueCat to manage purchases. RevenueCat knows you by an anonymous identifier that it creates, not by your name or email address. It processes your purchase history, your device type and system version, your IP address, and which purchase screens you saw and what you did on them. Our server asks RevenueCat whether your plan is active.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. For the record of purchase screens, our legitimate interest in understanding whether our offer works (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Suggesting your home airport

During setup, the app suggests a home airport. To do that it asks FreeIPAPI, a geolocation service, for the approximate location of your IP address, and picks the nearest airport. FreeIPAPI receives your IP address. We do not store the result, only the home airport you end up with, which stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> our legitimate interest in making setup easier (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can change the airport at any time.

### Weather, currency and country information

The app connects directly to two services, which see your IP address as any web server does. We send them no identifier.

<ul>
<li><strong>Open-Meteo:</strong> receives the coordinates of a destination in your flights, to give you its weather.</li>
<li><strong>ExchangeRate-API:</strong> provides currency rates. It receives no information about you beyond the request itself.</li>
</ul>

Country information comes from REST Countries through our server, which sends only a country code.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### On your iPhone

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. What these features read stays on your iPhone.

<ul>
<li><strong>Notifications:</strong> see <a href="#live-updates-and-alerts">Live updates and alerts</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Calendar:</strong> to add your flights to your calendar.</li>
<li><strong>Camera:</strong> to take a photo of a boarding pass or for your passport profile.</li>
<li><strong>Photos:</strong> to show the photos and videos taken during a trip as your trip memories, on a map if they have a location, and to save your passport card. Your library is never changed, and your photos are never sent to us. The choices you make, such as hiding a photo, sync through your private iCloud.</li>
<li><strong>Local network:</strong> to play games with people nearby.</li>
</ul>

The app does not ask for your location.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and never asks for permission to track you. You can withdraw a permission at any time in your iPhone's settings.

### Games

Nearby games connect directly to the other players' devices, and they see the player name you choose. Online games use Apple's Game Center, under your Game Center account.

### Crash reports

If the app crashes, Apple's diagnostics can give it a technical report, which stays on your iPhone. The app can then offer to email it to us. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it from your own email app. The report includes no flights, photos, contacts or location data, but we do receive your email address and whatever you add to the message.

<strong>Legal basis:</strong> your consent, given when you send the email (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

### If you write to us

If you email us, we use your email address and your message to answer you. <strong>Legal basis:</strong> our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### What you share

When you share a flight or your passport card, or export your data, you choose what is included and who gets it, using your iPhone's share sheet. It does not pass through our server.

## Who receives data

We share data only with the services below, and only for the reasons given in this policy.

<ul>
<li><strong>Cloudflare:</strong> hosts our server and its database.</li>
<li><strong>Apple:</strong> iCloud, push delivery, App Attest, App Store purchases, Game Center and Apple Maps.</li>
<li><strong>RevenueCat:</strong> manages in-app purchases.</li>
<li><strong>AeroDataBox</strong>, through the API.market marketplace: receives flight numbers, dates and airports from our server. It does not receive any identifier of you or your device.</li>
<li><strong>REST Countries:</strong> receives country codes from our server.</li>
<li><strong>FreeIPAPI, Open-Meteo and ExchangeRate-API:</strong> receive the requests described above directly from your iPhone. They are independent services, working under their own privacy terms.</li>
</ul>

We do not sell personal data.

## Where your data is stored, and data outside the EU

Our server and database run on Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare can process this data in countries outside the European Economic Area. Your iCloud data is stored by Apple according to your Apple Account.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, these safeguards apply:

<ul>
<li><strong>Cloudflare, Inc.</strong> (USA): the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).</li>
<li><strong>Apple Inc.</strong> (USA): standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).</li>
<li><strong>RevenueCat, Inc.</strong> (USA): standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).</li>
</ul>

To get a copy of the standard contractual clauses that apply, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool. The European Commission explains these safeguards on its [information page](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection_en).

## How long we keep data

<ul>
<li><strong>Your flights and trips:</strong> on your iPhone and in your iCloud until you delete them.</li>
<li><strong>A flight we track live:</strong> we delete it, with its Live Activity token and what you told us about it, when you delete the flight in the app, 24 hours after the flight is over, or at the latest about two days after its departure date.</li>
<li><strong>Technical delivery log:</strong> 14 days.</li>
<li><strong>Your device record:</strong> until Apple tells us that its push token no longer works, which we learn when we next try to reach it, or until you ask us to delete it.</li>
<li><strong>Rate limit counters:</strong> about a minute.</li>
<li><strong>Your plan:</strong> your customer record at RevenueCat stays until you ask us to remove it.</li>
<li><strong>Crash reports and emails:</strong> until the matter is dealt with.</li>
<li><strong>Purchase records at Apple:</strong> under Apple's own policies.</li>
</ul>

## What you must give us, and automated decisions

The app needs no personal data from you to work. Without notifications you do not get live alerts. No law requires you to give us personal data.

We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

<ul>
<li>get a copy of your data and information about how we use it (Art. 15);</li>
<li>have wrong data corrected (Art. 16);</li>
<li>have your data deleted (Art. 17);</li>
<li>have processing restricted (Art. 18);</li>
<li>receive your data in a machine-readable format and take it elsewhere (Art. 20);</li>
<li>withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what happened before (Art. 7(3)).</li>
</ul>

<strong>Your right to object.</strong> Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Email info (at) nfc (dot) cool.

We answer within one month. Because the app has no accounts, we cannot find your server records from your name or email address alone. If we cannot identify you, we will tell you, and you can give us information that lets us do so (Art. 11 GDPR).

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, in the EU country where you live or work or where you think your rights were infringed. In Portugal, where we are based, that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados ([cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt)).

## Export and delete your data

<strong>Export.</strong> In the app's settings you can export your flights and related data as a file.

<strong>Delete.</strong> Delete a flight in the app and it is removed from your iPhone, your iCloud and our server. If you delete the app, the copy in your iCloud stays until you remove it in your iPhone's iCloud settings. Your device record on our server is deleted as described in <a href="#how-long-we-keep-data">How long we keep data</a>.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store account settings. To have your customer record at RevenueCat deleted, email us.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. If you think someone younger than 16 has given us personal data, email info (at) nfc (dot) cool and we will delete it.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The app sets no cookies. It stores on your iPhone what it needs to work, such as your settings, your device identifier and the identifier RevenueCat uses for your purchases.

## Security

All connections to our servers are encrypted with HTTPS. The app signs its requests to our servers, and can prove with Apple's App Attest that it is the genuine app.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and fix problems quickly.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last modified" date at the top of this page. We do not start to use your data for a new purpose, or share it with a new kind of recipient, before this page describes it.
