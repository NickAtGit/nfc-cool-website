## What Is a Digital Business Card?

A digital business card is your contact details as a link: name, job, phone, email, website and social profiles on one page. Instead of handing over paper, you share it with an NFC tap or a QR code, and the other person saves you to their contacts in one tap. They don't need an app. When your number or job changes, you edit the card once, and every tag and QR code you've shared shows the new details.

## How to Make Your Digital Business Card

1. Get NFC.cool Business Card for iPhone or Android. It's free.
2. Fill in your card, or import a contact you already have. Add your logo and colours.
3. Share it: hold their phone to your NFC card or tag, show your QR code, send the link, or add it to Apple Wallet.

## NFC or QR?

Use both. An NFC tap is the fastest: they hold their phone near your card or tag and your card opens. A QR code works on any phone with a camera, also the ones without NFC. I compare the two in [NFC business card vs QR code](/blog/nfc-business-card-vs-qr-code/).

<h2 class="landing-section-title">Business Card FAQ</h2>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does the other person need the app?</summary>
<p>No. On iPhone your card opens in an App Clip, with nothing to install. On Android and computers it opens in the browser. Either way, they can save you as a contact.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What if their phone has no NFC?</summary>
<p>Show your QR code. Any phone camera can scan it.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What's free?</summary>
<p>One card, sharing by NFC, QR code or link, Save Contact, and Conference Mode's QR code. Your card shows "Made with NFC.cool".</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What do Personal and Pro cost?</summary>
<p>Personal is $19.99 a year and Pro is $49.99 a year, both with a one-week free trial. Personal adds leads and analytics and removes our name. Pro adds up to 10 cards.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>When do I need Team?</summary>
<p>When your company wants everyone on one brand, with every lead in one shared inbox and all the cards managed from the web. The 14-day trial needs a payment card, and you can start with two seats.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Which NFC tags work?</summary>
<p>Any writable NFC tag, like a sticker, card or key fob. Writing one is free, and because the tag links to your card, it stays up to date whenever you edit your card.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Who can see my card?</summary>
<p>Anyone with the link, and public cards can appear in search engines. A private card stays out of search and asks for a PIN. Your own NFC tags and QR codes already include the PIN, so people you share with go straight to your card.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can I export or delete my data?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can download everything as a ZIP, export leads as a spreadsheet, and delete your account.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>If I edit my card, do my tags and QR codes still work?</summary>
<p>Yes. Edits appear straight away on the same link, so there's nothing to rewrite or reprint. Deleting a card ends its link.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What happens if I cancel a paid plan?</summary>
<p>Nothing is deleted. On Personal or Pro your cards stay online: new leads stop, and only as many cards as your plan allows can be saved. The rest show as locked. On a team, once the subscription ends, your team's cards show a "temporarily unavailable" page until you resubscribe, and then it all comes back.</p>
</details>
