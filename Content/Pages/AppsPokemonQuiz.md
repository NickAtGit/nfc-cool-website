## The Story Behind It

This one exists because I kept losing PvP battles to type matchups I should have known. Flashcards felt like homework, so I turned the type chart into a quiz game instead - and it turns out playing your way through the matchups sticks much better than staring at a table.

It is a small app with a loyal following, and I keep it alive for exactly those people. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).
