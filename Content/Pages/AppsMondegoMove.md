# Mondego Move

An independent companion app for travelers using the Metro Mondego system in the Coimbra region: plan trips, browse the network map, and check fares - in English and Portuguese. Currently in beta on both platforms: open testing on Google Play and TestFlight on iOS.

## What It Does

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Trip planner</h3>
<p>Pick an origin and a destination and get your route through the Metro Mondego network.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Interactive map</h3>
<p>The whole network on one map, with station information a tap away. Location is used only to show your position - never stored or shared.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Fares and tickets</h3>
<p>Fare and ticket information in one place, so you know what your trip costs before you board.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Local listings</h3>
<p>Business and event listings around the network, submitted by the people who actually use the app.</p>
</article>

</div>

## The Story Behind It

I live part of the year in Portugal, and the Mondego region is home. When the Metro Mondego system arrived, I wanted a clean, bilingual companion for it - something a visitor could open in English and a local could use in Portuguese, with dark mode, tablet support, and offline capability built in from day one.

It is a small, honest utility: official data, an independent app, and a community layer of local listings on top. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).
