<h2 class="landing-section-title">Team FAQ</h2>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What is NFC.cool Business Card for Teams?</summary>
<p>It's the team plan of the NFC.cool Business Card app: digital business cards for everyone in a company, in one locked brand, with all their leads in one place and a web dashboard to run it. The plan comparison calls it Team.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How much does it cost?</summary>
<p>$50 per person a year, or $5 a month. In euros, the team price is the same number, and prices include VAT. The smallest team is two seats, so $100 a year or $10 a month.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How does the free trial work?</summary>
<p>It lasts 14 days and needs a payment card. You're billed when it ends unless you cancel. Each person gets one trial.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can we look around without a payment card?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can see your own card under your brand for 7 days with no payment card. Inviting your team starts with the trial.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How many cards does each person get?</summary>
<p>One to start with. An admin can allow up to 10 per person, with no extra charge.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Which CRMs does it work with?</summary>
<p>HubSpot connects directly: every new lead becomes a contact, with its notes and status. For any other system, a signed webhook sends each lead to an address you choose, which can be a Zapier or Make catch hook.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can we use single sign-on and directory sync?</summary>
<p>Single sign-on (SSO) and directory sync (SCIM) are set up on request. <a href="/contact/">Contact us</a> with what your company uses.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What happens to the leads when someone leaves?</summary>
<p>They stay with your team, and so does the card. A manager can hand them to someone else, and the person's seat is free again.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does the person we meet need an app?</summary>
<p>No. On iPhone a card opens in an App Clip, with nothing to install. On Android and computers it opens in the browser. Either way, they can save the contact.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do we need to buy NFC cards?</summary>
<p>No. Anyone can share by QR code or link. If you want something to tap, any writable NFC tag works, like a sticker, card or key fob.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can we use our own domain?</summary>
<p>Yes. Card links can run on a domain you own, such as cards.yourcompany.com. It comes with a paid team or a running trial.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Which AI assistants can connect?</summary>
<p>Any assistant that lets you add an MCP server with a token, such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. A token reads only unless you allow changes, reaches one team, and ends when you revoke it or leave the team.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What happens if we cancel?</summary>
<p>Nothing is deleted. Once the subscription ends, your team's cards show a "temporarily unavailable" page until you resubscribe, and then it all comes back.</p>
</details>
