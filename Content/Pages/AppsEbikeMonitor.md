## What It Does

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Live data monitor</h3>
<p>All your ride data on one screen: battery charge, speed, support mode, and cadence, with color visualization so you can read the support mode at a glance.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Live Activity</h3>
<p>Battery, support mode, speed, and cadence on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island - no unlocking mid-ride.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Last known charge</h3>
<p>The app remembers the state of charge when you disconnect from your bike, so you always know where the battery stands - even days later.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Battery management</h3>
<p>Set your battery and charger type and get an estimated time until full charge as a push notification when your bike disconnects.</p>
</article>

</div>

## The Story Behind It

I ride a Shimano-powered e-bike myself, and the data I wanted mid-ride was always two menu levels deep on the bike's own display. So I built the screen I wished I had: everything live, color-coded, and pinned to the Lock Screen through a Live Activity.

The battery features came from the same place - forgetting the state of charge after parking the bike for a week, and wondering how long a full charge would take before a tour. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).
