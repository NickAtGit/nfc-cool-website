## What It Does

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Track flights</h3>
<p>Add a flight from your booking or search the database. Live Activities follow you to the gate, and Home Screen widgets show your next departure and the time zone at your destination.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Build a passport</h3>
<p>Every flight adds a stamp to a holographic passport you can share. Earn achievements for the countries, airlines, and aircraft you have experienced.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Remember every trip</h3>
<p>Photos from your library surface automatically by date, mapped to the right trip. Relive every journey day by day after you land.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Explore offline</h3>
<p>Over 150 offline city guides built in, live exchange rates with daily updates, and Apple Intelligence itineraries for wherever you are headed next.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>At 30,000 ft</h3>
<p>Offline multiplayer games over Bluetooth - play with your seatmate or the friends a few rows up. No Wi-Fi required.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Offline first</h3>
<p>Add flights manually any time, free. Online flight search and live tracking are part of the subscription.</p>
</article>

</div>

<p class="app-note">Compatible with iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Apple Intelligence itineraries require iPhone 15 Pro or newer.</p>

## The Story Behind It

I fly a lot between Germany and Portugal, and I wanted the kind of flight app I would actually open after the trip, not just at the gate. So 1st Class keeps the tracking sharp - Live Activities, widgets, the whole modern iOS toolbox - and then turns everything you flew into something worth keeping: stamps, achievements, and the photos you took along the way.

It is my newest app and it moves fast; the roadmap is shaped directly by the people using it. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).
