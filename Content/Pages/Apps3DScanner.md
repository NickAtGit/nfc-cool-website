## What It Does

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Scan anything</h3>
<p>From trinkets on your desk to entire spaces - the LiDAR sensor captures real geometry, and you see the model build up live.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Preview in AR</h3>
<p>Place your scans back into the room with AR to get a real feel for how they stand in space before you export anything.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Export for print and design</h3>
<p>USDZ, STL, PLY, ABC, or OBJ - ready for 3D printing, game engines, and design tools. Exporting is part of the subscription.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Share and sync</h3>
<p>Grab a video of your 3D model for social media, and keep every scan in sync across your devices with iCloud.</p>
</article>

</div>

## The Story Behind It

3D and room scanning started as features inside NFC.cool Tools, and they turned out to be some of the most fun tech I have ever shipped. This standalone app is where that work lives on its own: one app, one job, everything tuned for capturing the world around you.

Hobbyists use it for 3D printing, designers use it to capture real-world references, and plenty of people just enjoy walking around their home turning things into models. If you want scanning as part of the bigger toolkit, the [3D Object Scanner](/features/3d-object-scanner/) and [Room Scanner](/features/room-scanner/) features in NFC.cool Tools do that too. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).

<h2 class="landing-section-title">3D Scanner FAQ</h2>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Which devices does it work on?</summary>
<p>It needs a LiDAR sensor, which Apple ships in the Pro devices: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, and LiDAR-equipped iPad Pro models.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Which formats can I export?</summary>
<p>USDZ, STL, PLY, ABC, and OBJ. STL goes straight into most 3D-printing slicers; OBJ and USDZ cover game engines and design tools. Exporting is available with the subscription.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can I use it for 3D printing?</summary>
<p>Yes. Scan an object, export it as STL, and load it into your slicer. Many people in the community use exactly this workflow for replacement parts and figurines.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do my scans sync between devices?</summary>
<p>Yes, iCloud Sync keeps your 3D models in sync across your iPhone and iPad.</p>
</details>
