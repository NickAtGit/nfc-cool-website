# Moments by NFC.cool

Tap the tag, open the album. Moments turns an NFC tag into a shared photo album: put it on the guest book, the back of a frame, or a gift, and everyone who taps it adds their photos to the same place.

## What It Does

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Connect a tag</h3>
<p>Write the album to an NFC tag and stick it on the thing the photos are about. A QR code opens the same album, for paper invitations and for phones without NFC.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Nothing to install</h3>
<p>Guests tap the tag and an App Clip opens right away. They add a photo and type a name if they want to. No download, no account.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>You decide what appears</h3>
<p>Nothing a guest adds is visible until you approve it. A report hides a photo immediately, and you can block someone from an album.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>A party camera</h3>
<p>Pick a look before the shutter, then add emoji and stickers as the photo goes in. Guests comment and react, still without an account.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>A poster of the night</h3>
<p>Turn the best shots into a collage poster when the party is over, and keep every album on your phone so it opens even without signal.</p>
</article>

</div>

<p class="app-note">Compatible with iPhone and iPad. Tapping an NFC tag needs an iPhone; on iPad, guests scan the QR code instead.</p>

</section>

## The Story Behind It

Every wedding I have been to ends the same way: the photos are spread across eleven phones and one group chat that dies within a week. Meanwhile the guest book sits on the table all night and everybody writes in it. I wanted the album to be as physical as the guest book, so the tag becomes the album.

It is my newest app, and the one where NFC does the most work for people who have never heard of it. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).

<h2 class="landing-section-title">Moments FAQ</h2>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do my guests need the app?</summary>
<p>No. Tapping the tag or scanning the QR code opens an App Clip, so guests can add a photo without installing anything or creating an account. Only the host needs the app.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is Moments free?</summary>
<p>An album that lives on your phone is free. Putting it on the internet, where the tag and your guests can reach it, is the part you pay for: once for a single album, or a monthly subscription that covers every album you make.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can anyone post anything?</summary>
<p>No. Nothing a guest adds is visible until you approve it. Anyone can report a photo or a comment, which hides it immediately, and you can block a person from an album.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Which devices does it run on?</summary>
<p>iPhone and iPad. Guests need an iPhone to tap the NFC tag; everyone else, iPad included, scans the QR code.</p>
</details>
