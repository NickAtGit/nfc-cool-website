## Why a Standalone QR App

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Fast, accurate scanning</h3>
<p>Real-time detection reads codes the moment they enter the frame. URLs, text, contacts - the content opens with a single tap.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Create your own codes</h3>
<p>Turn any content into a QR code. The QR Code Studio adds extensive customization on top, so your codes can match your brand.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>History that stays organized</h3>
<p>Every scan is saved and organized for easy access. Share a code or its content with others whenever you need it again.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Batch and multi-scan</h3>
<p>Scan a stack of codes in one session with batch mode, or keep the camera running with continuous multi-scan.</p>
</article>

</div>

<p class="app-note">Scanning is free. QR Code Studio, batch mode, and multi-scan are paid features - each can be tried before buying (Studio also works free with a watermark).</p>

## The Story Behind It

QR scanning has been part of NFC.cool Tools for years, and I kept hearing the same thing: some people just want the QR part. No NFC, no document scanning, no 3D - just a really good QR scanner. So I built exactly that, with the same care for detail as the main toolkit.

If you want QR codes plus everything else, [NFC.cool Tools](/features/qr-scanner/) has you covered. If you want QR codes and nothing else, this is your app. Browse the rest of the lineup on the [apps page](/apps/).

</section>

<h2 class="landing-section-title">QR Code Scanner FAQ</h2>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is the app free?</summary>
<p>Yes, scanning QR codes is free. The QR Code Studio, batch scanning, and continuous multi-scan are paid features, and each one can be evaluated with a free trial first.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can it create QR codes too?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can create QR codes from your own content, and the QR Code Studio adds extensive visual customization for codes that need to look on-brand.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How is this different from NFC.cool Tools?</summary>
<p>NFC.cool Tools is the full toolkit: NFC, QR, barcodes, documents, 3D, and room scanning in one app. QR Code Scanner is the QR half on its own, for people who do not need the rest.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does it work on iPad?</summary>
<p>Yes, the app supports both iPhone and iPad, including dark mode on both.</p>
</details>
