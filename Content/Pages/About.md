NFC.cool is an independent product from its founder, **Nicolo Stanciu**, built between Germany and Portugal. The mission: make NFC accessible for the rest of us. It's a famously nerdy technology and most apps for it are too technical - so NFC.cool sets out to be the best mobile scanning toolkit anyone has, built for everyone.

## The story

NFC.cool started as a personal itch. Apple opened up NFC on iOS in 2017, and I was one of the first to build for it - I shipped the first version of NFC.cool Tools that year, put it on the App Store, and watched a small but passionate community form around it.

NFC.cool has since grown into a family of apps: the full scanning toolkit (NFC, QR, Barcodes, Documents, 3D, Room Scans, Webhooks), the dedicated Business Card app on iPhone and Android, and an Android build of the toolkit focused on what Android does best - together carrying {{RATINGS_TOTAL}}+ ratings at {{RATINGS_AVG}} stars across the App Store and Google Play. Everything is still built by its founder, between Germany and Portugal.

I keep it that way on purpose. NFC.cool isn't VC-backed, so the people I answer to are the ones using the apps, and I use what I ship every day.

## What NFC.cool stands for

<div class="page-cards-grid">

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Privacy by default</h3>
<p>Tags you read and write, documents, passports and ID cards, 3D models: the app processes them on your device, and their contents don't reach me. If you turn on webhooks, scan data goes straight to <em>your</em> server. For what the app does share, such as analytics, crash reports, ads and purchases, see the <a href="/privacy/">privacy policy</a>.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>On-device processing</h3>
<p>OCR, photogrammetry, NFC parsing - all run locally. No cloud round-trips, no internet required.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Open standards</h3>
<p>NFC Forum types, NDEF, vCard, iCalendar, OpenPrintTag. Where standards exist, NFC.cool follows them.</p>
</article>

<article class="page-card">
<h3>Free where it can be</h3>
<p>Reading NFC tags, scanning QR codes and barcodes, document scanning, 3D and room scanning - the core toolkit is free to use. The free tier is ad-supported; an optional Platinum subscription removes the ads and unlocks the advanced workflows. The essentials never cost anything.</p>
</article>
