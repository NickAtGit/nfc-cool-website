## Who we are and what these terms cover

These terms are an agreement between you and Nicolo Stanciu, trading as NFC.cool, Rua Carminé Nobre 6, 3040-193 Coimbra, Portugal, NIF PT315901934, email business (at) nfc (dot) cool ("NFC.cool", "we", "us").

They cover the NFC.cool partner programme: your partner link, setting up teams for clients, the demo, the share kit and the sales guides in your partner area at business.nfc.cool, and the commission you earn on the teams you bring to NFC.cool Business Card for Teams.

Commissions are paid by Creem, not by us. When a team you bring buys on the web, Creem sells it to the team as the merchant of record, and Creem's affiliate programme pays your share. Creem's own terms for its affiliate programme apply to that. [CHECK WITH CREEM: the name and link of Creem's affiliate terms, to cite here.]

You accept these terms when you join the programme. Your NFC.cool account itself stays under the [Business Card terms](/terms/business-card/).

<ul>
<li>Anyone with an NFC.cool account can join, from any country.</li>
<li>Accounts of the NFC.cool Tools app cannot join. Use an account at business.nfc.cool or in the Business Card app.</li>
<li>Joining is free and needs no approval. [CHECK WITH CREEM: a minimum age, and whether Creem accepts affiliates from every country.]</li>

## Joining, and getting ready to sell

<ol>
<li>Open business.nfc.cool/partners/join/. Without an account, enter your email address: we send you a link that signs you in and makes your account. With an account, sign in with it.</li>
<li>Tell us your country and what kind of partner you are. Your website or profile is optional.</li>
<li>Creem invites you to its affiliate programme by email, at your account's confirmed email address. Accept that invite.</li>
</ol>

Until you have accepted Creem's invite, nothing you do can be credited to you, so your partner area waits for it: your link, Set up a team and the demo address with your code are shown once Creem has linked you. Your link opens the Teams page in that time, but it remembers no one. The guides, the share kit texts and the plain demo are yours to read at once.

## What you earn

You earn **20%** of every payment a team you bring makes on Creem for its NFC.cool Business Card for Teams subscription, for as long as it pays.

<ul>
<li>Creem works out your share from the price the team pays on Creem, VAT included. [CHECK WITH CREEM: confirm the share is of the VAT-included price, and how discounts and promotion codes change it.]</li>
<li>"For as long as it pays" means every renewal, monthly or yearly, and every change of seats. [CHECK WITH CREEM: the programme is set to pay on every renewal, with no end date.]</li>
<li>A new team starts with a free trial. Nothing is paid during the trial, so it earns nothing. Your share starts with the first payment.</li>
<li>Prices depend on the country of the business, so the same team earns a different amount in different countries. Your share is in the currency the team pays in.</li>
<li>If a payment is refunded or charged back, the share it earned goes too. [CHECK WITH CREEM: how and when Creem takes back a commission on a refund or a chargeback, including one already paid out.]</li>
</ul>

## Founding deals

The first founding deals across the whole programme earn **40%** instead of 20%, for as long as the team pays.

<ul>
<li><strong>A place belongs to a team, not to a partner.</strong> If you bring 50 founding deals, all 50 earn you 40%.</li>
<li><strong>How many there are.</strong> We start with 100 places. How many are left is shown on the partner page and in your partner area. We may add places at any time. If we ever lower the number, a team that holds a place keeps it.</li>
<li><strong>When a team takes a place.</strong> When the team's checkout opens on Creem and a place is free, the team takes it, and its subscription is set up at 40% from the start. It stays at 40% when the team changes its seats or switches between monthly and yearly billing.</li>
<li><strong>Held, then final.</strong> The place is held until the team's first payment. From then on it is the team's for good.</li>
<li><strong>When a held place goes back.</strong> It goes back to the programme if the checkout is not completed within a day, if the trial ends without a payment, or if the first payment is refunded or disputed. A refund or dispute of a later payment does not take the place back.</li>
<li><strong>A checkout completed late.</strong> If a checkout is completed after its place went back for not being completed in time, the team still earns 40%.</li>
<li><strong>At most 5 held places per partner.</strong> While 5 of your teams hold a place and have not paid yet, the next team you bring is sold at 20%, and its subscription stays at 20%. As soon as one of the 5 pays or gives its place back, your next team can take a place again.</li>
<li><strong>Once all places are taken,</strong> new teams earn 20%. Teams that hold a place keep their 40%.</li>
</ul>

## How a team is credited to you

A team is credited to you in one of two ways, and each team is credited to one partner only.

### Your link

Your link is business.nfc.cool/partners/r/ followed by your code. It opens the Teams page on nfc.cool, in the visitor's language.

<ul>
<li>Opening it remembers you in that browser for 30 days. If the business then starts a team there and buys it, the team is yours.</li>
<li>If the business opens another partner's link later, the last link opened counts.</li>
<li>One opening of your link credits one purchase. For a second team, the business opens your link again.</li>
<li>Your link is remembered by a cookie in the browser. If the business buys in another browser or on another device, or after clearing or blocking cookies, we cannot credit the team to you.</li>
<li>A team that is already credited to a partner stays with that partner.</li>
<li>The demo address in your partner area carries your code, and remembers you in the same way when it is opened.</li>
</ul>

### Setting up a team and handing it over

You set up the team for your client and hand it over. See the next section.

## Setting up a team for a client

<ul>
<li>In your partner area, Set up a team makes a new team in your client's name. You are its owner while you build it: its cards, its brand, its members.</li>
<li>When it is ready, you make a take-over link and send it to your client. Only you can make it. It works once, for 7 days. A new link cancels the one before.</li>
<li>You cannot use the take-over link yourself.</li>
<li>When your client takes the team over, they become its only owner and you leave the team. Everything set up while you ran it stops reaching it: other owners and admins become members (your client can promote them again), webhooks stop, other people's access tokens stop working, open invitations and ownership offers are cancelled, and HubSpot and Salesforce are disconnected.</li>
<li>Your client then chooses the seats and pays. If they never had a team trial, the first 14 days are free.</li>
<li>A team is handed over once. After that it is never yours to hand over again, and it stays credited to you.</li>
</ul>

## What never earns a commission

<ul>
<li>Purchases in the App Store or Google Play.</li>
<li>Payments a team makes in any other way than on Creem.</li>
<li>Your own purchases, and purchases made in a browser where you are signed in as yourself.</li>
<li>A team you are a member of when it buys. That is your own business, not a team you brought.</li>
<li>A team bought before you accepted Creem's invite.</li>
<li>A team that is already credited to another partner.</li>
</ul>

## What you see about the teams you bring

Your partner area lists every team you set up or brought, with:

<ul>
<li>its name and its stage: being set up, trial, paying, payment failed, or ended;</li>
<li>its dates: when it started, when its trial ends, since when it pays, when a payment failed or when it ended;</li>
<li>about what it earns you a month, and at which rate, 20% or 40%;</li>
<li>Creem's figures for you: what is pending, approved and paid, per currency.</li>
</ul>

While you set a team up you see all of it, as its owner. A team bought through your link, or one your client has taken over, is not yours to open: you see only what is listed above, never its cards, members, contacts or billing.

The amounts in your partner area are estimates made from the team's price. What Creem reports, and pays, is what counts.
